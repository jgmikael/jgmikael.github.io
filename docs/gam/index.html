This document describes the General Authorisation Model, i.e., the Vocabulary serving both Nordic DQS work as well as EU WE BUILD LSP..

Alternate versions of the vocabulary definition exist in Turtle and JSON-LD.

Published:
2026-09-29
Version Info:
Early draft, 2026-09-29
See Also:

Working publication of the early General Authorisation Model (GAM) draft. The source model has known deficiencies. Domains and ranges marked in the source YAML as inferred from existential restrictions are editorial publication hints; the generated RDF is not a lossless reproduction of the source OWL. The canonical term identifiers retain the https://iri.suomi.fi/model/authz/ namespace.

Source: GAM YAML. Original draft: GAM OWL namespace. Four object-property ranges and several XSD datatype ranges are editorial proposals needed for generation; they require review before authoritative use.
