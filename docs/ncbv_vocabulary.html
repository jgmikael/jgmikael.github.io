RepresentationRule

Representation Rule (Signatory Rule)
A rule that describes who or which agents a mandate is granted to. The rule can be used both in the case of legal representation, when a CEO or a board member signs for the agent (company) based on their official role in the company, as well as when a mandate is given to a specific person ("a man on the street") who has no previous affiliation to the mandating agent.
